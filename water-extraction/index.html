Before anything else in a restoration project can work, the water has to be extracted. The faster standing water is removed from your property, the less damage occurs to structural materials, flooring, personal belongings and building systems. Every hour that water sits on surfaces, it penetrates deeper into materials, soaking through carpet into padding, wicking up drywall, saturating wood framing and seeping into subflooring. Across San Francisco, San Francisco Restoration handles emergency extraction with industrial portable and truck-mounted machines that can remove hundreds of gallons a minute.

Our truck-mounted extraction units are among the most powerful water removal systems available, with the suction to pull water from carpets, hard floors and structural materials far more effectively than consumer or rental equipment. For areas truck-mounted hoses cannot reach, we deploy powerful portable extractors, submersible pumps and specialty tools designed for specific situations, including water trapped in wall cavities, between floor layers and in confined spaces.

There is more to a professional extraction than getting rid of the water in plain sight. Water migrates behind baseboards, below flooring, into wall cavities and under cabinets, and our technicians, who are IICRC-certified, locate it with moisture detection tools. The more moisture our specialty tools take from these concealed areas ahead of drying, the shorter and lighter the structural drying job becomes, and the lower the chance of secondary mold growth.

What a Professional Brings to Water Extraction

Both the harm done to your home and the restoration bill depend directly on quick, thorough extraction. Truck-mounted extractors move water at hundreds of times the rate of consumer tools, and a job that could cost you a full day with a shop vac is done by our machines in minutes. Speed matters for more than comfort: every extra hour of water sitting against building materials can mean thousands of dollars more in damage.

Our extraction also goes after moisture that home equipment can neither find nor get to. Water travels by capillary action into carpet pad, wicks up drywall, runs along electrical conduits and plumbing lines, and gets between flooring layers. Surface extraction by itself leaves much of this behind, so our team uses specialty techniques and tools aimed at the hidden moisture. The payoff is a shorter drying period, lower equipment cost and, most important of all, less risk of mold growing unseen in trapped moisture.

Signs It Is Time for Water Extraction

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • A utility area, mechanical room or crawl space with water in it
  • A basement taking on water after heavy rain or a sump pump failure
  • Water sitting on a floor anywhere in the building, no matter where it came from
  • Active leaks from plumbing, appliances or HVAC systems
  • Moisture appearing along the bottom of walls or at cabinet toe kicks
  • Seams in the floor that ooze water under foot pressure
  • Ceilings, light fixtures or walls that are actively leaking water

Water Extraction Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Keep a basic emergency water kit: old towels, a bucket and your restoration company's number
  • Consider an automatic shutoff valve, which senses a leak and closes the water supply
  • Save (610) 552-3627 in your contacts now, and you can reach us at once when something floods
  • Find the main shutoff valve for the house and put a clear label on it
  • Critical areas of the house should each have a leak sensor that you know is working
  • Early leak signs show up near appliances, toilets and sink cabinets, so check those spots on a schedule
