Wiping away the mold you can see is not remediation. Our comprehensive approach means mold is eliminated at the source, not just hidden for a while.

Mold Remediation Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly mold issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Make sure crawl spaces are properly ventilated and consider installing a vapor barrier
  • When windows, piping or other cold surfaces start to sweat, correct it without waiting
  • Use ventilation and a dehumidifier to hold humidity under 60%, with 30 to 50% as the ideal
  • Clear gutters and downspouts regularly, which keeps rain from soaking the foundation walls
  • Use mold-resistant drywall and paint in moisture-prone areas like bathrooms and basements
  • Check that exhaust fans in the kitchen, laundry room and bathrooms move enough air

The Benefits of Professional Mold Remediation

Removing mold is work homeowners should not attempt on their own. Disturbing mold colonies without proper containment sends millions of spores airborne, which can contaminate unaffected areas of your home and create worse health exposure than the original problem. Our crews work in personal protective equipment inside a negative-pressure enclosure, with HEPA air scrubbers running, to trap spores and hold them in the work area while removal is underway.

Just as important, we deal with moisture, the underlying cause of every mold problem. Finding concealed moisture, drying it properly and preventing a repeat all take equipment and training, which certified remediation technicians have.

Signs It Is Time for Mold Remediation

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Black, green, white or other colored growth you can see on surfaces in the building
  • Belongings in storage, including clothes and cardboard, showing spots or fuzz
  • Recent water damage or flooding that was not professionally dried
  • Feeling better away from home, then having coughs, headaches or allergies come back indoors
  • Windows, cold surfaces or walls that sweat with condensation on a regular basis
  • Stains spreading on a ceiling or wall, or dark marks ringing the air vents
  • A slow leak under a sink, behind an appliance or in the roof that went unnoticed
