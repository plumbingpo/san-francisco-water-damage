Few emergencies in a building are as hazardous as sewage backing up into it. Anyone who comes in contact with contaminated material is exposed to parasites, bacteria and viruses, among them giardia, E. coli, salmonella and hepatitis, all carried in raw sewage and all dangerous. When it happens in San Francisco, San Francisco Restoration is available 24/7 with technicians trained for this work, proper protective gear and disinfectants registered with the EPA, everything needed for a safe decontamination.

Sewage damage is classified as Category 3 (black water), the most contaminated and dangerous category of water damage. That rating calls for the strictest protocols: containment barriers go up, crews wear full protective equipment, every porous material the sewage touched is removed, and the surfaces that stay are disinfected thoroughly. We work to the IICRC S500 standard that governs Category 3 losses, with the goal of returning your property to a condition safe to occupy.

We clear the standing sewage first, then pull contaminated water out of structural materials, remove carpet, padding, insulation, drywall and any other porous item that was in contact with sewage for proper disposal, disinfect each hard surface, treat with antimicrobial products and finish with structural drying, which prevents secondary mold. To finish, thermal foggers and hydroxyl generators of industrial grade deodorize the space so the sewage smell does not linger.

Why Hire a Professional for Sewage Cleanup?

No homeowner should try to clean up sewage personally. Handling raw sewage safely takes specialized training, EPA-registered disinfectants that consumers cannot buy, and personal protective equipment, meaning respirators, Tyvek suits and protective boots and gloves. People exposed to the pathogens in sewage can become seriously ill with skin and respiratory infections, hepatitis or gastrointestinal disease.

Contaminated materials count as biohazard waste, and disposing of them properly is part of a professional cleanup. Simply placing sewage-contaminated drywall, carpet and insulation in your regular trash is both illegal and hazardous to sanitation workers and the community. Certified restoration companies maintain proper waste disposal protocols and relationships with licensed biohazard waste disposal facilities.

Sewage Cleanup Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Keep cooking oil, grease and food scraps out of the drain, where they build into clogs
  • Have your sewer line inspected with a camera every 2 to 3 years to catch potential problems
  • Hair and other debris are easy to stop with simple strainers on tub, shower and sink drains
  • Address slow drains promptly, before they become complete blockages
  • Find your sewer cleanout now and check its condition, so it can be reached in an emergency
  • A backwater valve at the sewer connection stops the city system from backing up into your home

How to Tell You Need Sewage Cleanup

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Carpet or flooring contaminated when a toilet overflowed with solid waste
  • More than one drain in the house overflowing or backing up together
  • Unexplained illness or gastrointestinal symptoms among household members
  • Foul-smelling moisture showing up on flooring or along the walls
  • Sewage or dark water backing up through floor drains, toilets or tubs
  • Sewage residue visible around drain openings or on basement floors
  • Wastewater seeping from the sewer cleanout or pooling in the yard above the line
